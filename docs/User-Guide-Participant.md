# SprintPulse — Participant User Guide

## Overview

As a participant, you respond to polls and contribute to retro boards during live sessions such as workshops, retrospectives, and PI events. This guide explains how to access a poll or retro using your team PIN, submit your responses, and what to expect during a session.

---

## Accessing Your Team's Polls

### Using a Team PIN

1. Navigate to the app and click **Participant** (e.g., `https://sprintpulse-app.vercel.app`)
2. Enter the **team PIN** provided by your facilitator (e.g., "A3K7")
3. Click **Join Team**
4. You'll see a list of polls available for your team

**Note:** Your team PIN is remembered in your browser — you won't need to re-enter it next time.

### Switching Teams

- Click the **Switch Team** button at the top of the poll list
- Enter a different team PIN to see another team's polls

---

## Joining a Poll

1. From the poll list, click on the poll you want to participate in
2. Enter your **display name** (2–50 characters)
3. Click **Continue**

Your name is stored in your browser for convenience.

---

## Answering Questions

Once you enter your name, the poll questions appear:

- **Desktop:** Questions may be arranged visually on a canvas background
- **Mobile:** Questions appear as a vertical scrollable list

### For each question:

1. **Select one option** from the predefined choices
2. If a **"Custom"** option is available, select it and type your own answer
3. All questions must be answered before submitting

---

## Submitting Your Responses

1. After answering all questions, click **Submit**
2. A confirmation screen appears: "Thank you for your responses!"
3. Your responses are saved — you cannot edit them after submission

**Important:** You can only submit once per poll from the same browser.

### After a Reset

If the facilitator resets the poll:
- You'll see "Already submitted" until you refresh
- Click **"Poll was reset? Click to check again"** or refresh the page
- You can then re-enter your name and submit again

---

## Participating in a Retro

Retro boards are collaborative spaces where your team reflects on what's working and what needs to change. The facilitator guides the session through three phases.

---

### Joining a Retro

1. Navigate to the app and click **Participant**
2. Enter the **team PIN** provided by your facilitator (or use a direct link/QR code)
3. Click **Join Team**
4. Select the retro from your team's activity list
5. Enter your **display name** and click **Continue**

---

### Gather Phase

During the Gather phase, you contribute cards to the board:

1. Click **+ Add Card** on any lane (e.g., "Start", "Stop", "Continue")
2. Type your thought in the text field and press **Enter** or click **Add**
3. You can add as many cards as you like to any lane
4. To edit a card you wrote, click on it and update the text
5. To delete a card you wrote, click the delete icon on the card

**Privacy:** Cards are anonymous by default. You can see your own cards, but other participants' cards appear only as a count per lane until the Vote phase begins.

---

### Vote Phase

Once the facilitator advances to the Vote phase:

1. All cards across all lanes become visible to everyone
2. Vote on cards you find important by clicking the **thumbs-up** icon
3. You have a limited number of votes (set by the facilitator) — spend them wisely
4. You can remove a vote by clicking the thumbs-up icon again on a card you previously voted on

Your remaining vote count is displayed on screen.

---

### Closed Phase

When the facilitator closes the retro:

1. The board becomes read-only — you cannot add cards or votes
2. **Action items** are displayed, showing what the team committed to
3. Each action item includes a description, assignee, and optional due date
4. The facilitator may share an export (PDF or JSON) with the team

---

### Visual Features

- **Lane icons and colors** — each column has a distinct icon and color for easy identification
- **Background images** — the facilitator may set a themed background
- **Phase indicator** — a banner at the top shows the current phase (Gather, Vote, or Closed) so you always know what actions are available

---

## What Happens During a Live Session

### Response Collection Opens and Closes

- The facilitator controls when response collection is open
- If response collection is **closed**, you'll see: "Response collection is currently closed"
- If response collection **closes while you're filling in the form**, the submit button is disabled

### Results

- The facilitator decides when and how results are revealed
- Results may be shown on a shared screen during the session
- If anonymisation is enabled, your name won't appear in results
- If anonymisation is disabled and results are set to **DETAILS**, your name will be visible next to your responses (both predefined option selections and custom text)

---

## Tips

- Get your team PIN from your facilitator before the session starts
- Answer promptly once response collection opens — the facilitator may close it at any time
- Use the "Custom" option to share unique perspectives
- If you lose connection, the app retries automatically

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "PIN not recognized" | Double-check the PIN with your facilitator |
| "Response collection is currently closed" | Wait for the facilitator to open response collection |
| "Already submitted" | Click "check again" or refresh after a reset |
| Page won't load | Check your internet connection and refresh |
| Submit button disabled | Ensure all questions are answered |

---

## Privacy

- Your responses are stored securely
- The facilitator can enable anonymisation to hide your name from results
- Response data is automatically purged after 90 days
- Your session token prevents duplicate submissions
