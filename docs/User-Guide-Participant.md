# Shoprite-X Polling App — Participant User Guide

## Overview

As a participant, you respond to polls during live sessions such as workshops, retrospectives, and PI events. This guide explains how to access a poll, submit your responses, and what to expect during a session.

---

## Accessing a Poll

1. Your facilitator will share a **poll URL** (via chat, email, or screen share)
2. Open the link in your browser
3. The poll title and description are displayed

---

## Entering Your Name

1. Type your **display name** (2–50 characters) in the name field
2. Click **Continue**
3. Your name is stored in your browser — if you refresh the page, it will be restored automatically

**Note:** Your name is used to identify your responses. If anonymisation is enabled by the facilitator, your real name will not be shown in results.

---

## Answering Questions

Once you enter your name, the poll questions appear. Depending on the device:

- **Desktop:** Questions may be arranged visually on a canvas background
- **Mobile:** Questions appear as a vertical scrollable list

### For each question:

1. **Select one option** from the predefined choices
2. If a **"Custom"** option is available, you can select it and type your own answer
3. All questions must be answered before submitting

### Validation Rules

- Every question must have a selection
- If you select "Custom", the text field cannot be empty
- Your name must be at least 2 characters

---

## Submitting Your Responses

1. After answering all questions, click **Submit**
2. A **confirmation screen** appears: "Already submitted"
3. Your responses are saved — you cannot edit them after submission

**Important:** You can only submit once per poll from the same browser. If you try again, you'll see the "Already submitted" message.

---

## What Happens During a Live Session

### Voting Opens and Closes

- The facilitator controls when voting is open
- If voting is **closed**, you'll see: "Voting is currently closed"
- If voting **closes while you're filling in the form**:
  - The submit button is disabled
  - A banner appears: "Voting has been closed by the facilitator"
  - Your partial answers are **not saved**
  - You can still copy your free-text answers before navigating away

### Results

- The facilitator decides when and how results are revealed
- You may see results on a shared screen during the session
- If anonymisation is enabled, your name won't appear in results

---

## Tips

- Have your poll URL ready before the session starts
- Answer promptly once voting opens — the facilitator may close it at any time
- Use the "Custom" option to share unique perspectives not covered by predefined choices
- If you lose connection, the app will retry automatically and show a "Connection lost" banner

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "Voting is currently closed" | Wait for the facilitator to open voting |
| "Already submitted" | You've already responded — each person can only submit once |
| Page won't load | Check your internet connection and try refreshing |
| Submit button disabled | Ensure all questions are answered; check if voting was closed |
| Name field shows previous name | This is normal — your name is remembered from your last session |

---

## Privacy

- Your responses are stored securely in the database
- The facilitator can enable anonymisation to hide your name from results
- Response data is automatically purged after 90 days
- Your session token (stored in your browser) prevents duplicate submissions
